# MMS-05 — Proposed applicant privacy notice and consent policy

**REVIEW DRAFT — NOT APPROVED, NOT PUBLICATION-READY.** This document is a proposal for the owner's decision. The application must keep real applicant submissions and media publication disabled until the final notice, consent-version identifier, retention/deletion terms and request contact are approved, implemented and tested.

## Plain-language proposed notice to applicants

MMS operates a timed performance competition. If you choose to apply, MMS may collect your contact details, stage name and preferred name, application and event choices, headshots, résumé, demo reel, representation and union-status information, and your choices about consent. Technical records may include submission time, authentication activity and private review history. Do not upload materials that you do not own or have permission to provide.

MMS would use your submission only to assess competition eligibility, review your application, administer the selected event, send necessary participation notices, honor your permissions and investigate misuse. Private application information would be available only to authorized application reviewers and operational staff. A verified industry account would **not** gain access to private applicant content merely by being verified.

Public use of a photograph, performance, résumé extract, stage name, recording, clip, promotional material or profile would require a separate, specific and recorded permission. Consent to apply is **not** consent to publish or to use applicant media in marketing, create AI training data, transfer your likeness to third parties, or sell personal information. Withdrawal must disable future optional uses, subject to documented prior distribution, legally necessary retention and contracts that have been separately agreed.

MMS would keep only information needed for the declared purposes, apply role-based access and encrypted transport/storage where supported, and permit requests for access, correction, withdrawal and deletion through **[OWNER-APPROVED PRIVACY CONTACT REQUIRED]**. Any third-party infrastructure, retention periods, disclosure jurisdictions and legally required rights notices must be stated in the approved final version. The service must not present unsupported security or privacy guarantees.

## Separate consent choices (all unselected by default)

| Permission | Required to submit? | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Process necessary application and privately review media | Yes, with an explicit notice acknowledgement | No submission until acknowledged |
| Contact about this application or event | Necessary for event administration only | No promotional marketing |
| Publish selected performance or actor profile publicly | No | Off |
| Permit verified industry discovery/contact | No | Off |
| Use media in unrelated promotion or archive | No; requires separately scoped license | Off |
| Share data for AI training, biometric extraction or model inference | No | Prohibited unless separately designed and approved |

Each acknowledgement must record policy version, exact text or content hash, choice, purpose, timestamp, authorized account, and subsequent revocation. Do not combine different purposes in one irrevocable checkbox.

## Engineering acceptance, not yet achieved

Submissions remain disabled until the approved notice is wired into server-side submission authorization; consent purpose/version is retained in an append-only audit record; media is private by default with tested signed access, expiry and revocation; no verified-industry role can silently read private content; automated deletion actually enforces each approved retention category; notifications follow the distinct consent purposes; and applicant withdrawal/access/deletion are tested against both storage and search indexes.

This proposal makes no statement that an actual applicant has consented, that MMS is live, that media infrastructure exists, or that any vendor or regulator has approved the policy.
