# MMS-05 actor application preflight (partial)

Pure, client-independent validator accepts only tenant/event/applicant IDs,
explicit consent revision, private content digests and bounded representation
and union-status categories. The projection explicitly disallows publication
or industry discovery by default.

No actual files are uploaded and no private application is saved, submitted or
published. Digests alone do not establish malware safety or data authenticity.
Missing work includes owner-authorized privacy policy, secure encrypted storage,
verified applicant identity, applicant consent UI, submissions and withdrawal,
virus scanning, legal retention/export/delete, private reviewer access, testing
and actual Live Competition Engine integration.
